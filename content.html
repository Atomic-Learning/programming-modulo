The modulo operator returns the remainder of a division operation. For example, 13 modulo 5 evaluates to 3 because 13 divided by 5 is 2 with a remainder of 3 (i.e. $2 \times 5 + 3 =13$), Some more examples are below:

Dividend Divisor Result
10 3 1
17 5 2
9 3 0

In the cases above, all values are positive integers and how they are handled is the same across almost all programming languages. Many, but not all, programming languages support using floating point numbers for one or more of the inputs. Different languages will have slightly different behaviours when one or more of the inputs are negative.
